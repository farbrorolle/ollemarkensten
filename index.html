I’m a Swedish platinum music producer, songwriter, composer and musician living and working in Copenhagen.

I’m currently producing and writing for top artists across the world, and playing for some of the most well known artists in Scandinavia, as well as composing for TV and other media. I have a masters degree in Music Design from Royal College of Music at Stockholm.

Live — keyboardist and musical director

With over 20 years of experience as a live keyboardist and musical director, I’ve worked with artists such as Tove Lo, Icona Pop, Carola, Lasse Skriver, Liive, and Emma Essinger.

Today, my primary focus is full-time music production and composition, bringing two decades of live and stage experience into the studio. I continue to accept select bookings for solo piano performances and band shows. For inquiries, see contact info below.
